South Africa

South Africa is located at the southern tip of Africa and is known for its rich cultural diversity, beautiful landscapes, and historical significance. It is home to famous attractions such as Table Mountain, Kruger National Park, and the Cape of Good Hope. South Africa is often called the "Rainbow Nation" because of its many cultures and languages.
